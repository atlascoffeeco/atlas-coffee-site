Before you write

The usual questions.

Can we try it before we order?

Yes, if you are nearby. I can bring coffee so you can taste it on your machine, or we can do a short session on the bar. That is often the best first step.

Do you grind it?

Café bags come whole bean, so you grind on site.

How does a drop work?

I roast for your day, then drop locally or you collect in Redditch. Weekly or fortnightly is typical. Local drop is by arrangement, not a courier run.

How do we order once we are set up?

A message or email is enough. There is no wholesale login. Tell me the kilos and the day you need it, and I roast for that drop.

Enquire

Tell me about the site.

Café, church, office or deli: say where you are and what you are trying to do. I usually reply within 1-2 working days. If you are nearby I can bring coffee to try before you commit to a first order.
