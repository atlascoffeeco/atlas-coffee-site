The short version

Atlas keeps things deliberately focused: thoughtful coffees, clear descriptions, careful roasting, and a simple way to buy fresh coffee for delivery or local collection.

Meet the roaster

A coffee business kept close to the cup.

I’m the founder and roaster behind Atlas Coffee. My experience as a barista shaped the standards behind the business and taught me that great coffee does not need to be complicated or performative.

Atlas began with a simple aim: to make carefully chosen, well-roasted coffee that feels welcoming to drink and easy to return to.

Why Atlas exists

Proper standards without the unnecessary noise.

I wanted to build the kind of coffee business I would genuinely want to buy from myself: thoughtful, honest, and focused on quality without the gimmicks.

That means keeping the range focused, describing each coffee clearly, and staying close to the decisions that shape the final cup.

What you can expect

Simple standards, held properly.

Atlas is built around the things that matter when you are actually drinking the coffee: flavour, consistency, clarity, and an experience that feels straightforward.

Small batches, more control

More attention at every stage.

Roasting at a considered scale makes it possible to taste, adjust, and repeat each coffee with care rather than rushing towards volume.

Flavour you can live with

Balanced, sweet, and easy to enjoy.

Atlas coffees are selected for clarity, sweetness, and drinkability —not simply for impressive tasting-note lists.

Straightforward by design

Clear information, no posturing.

You should be able to understand what a coffee tastes like, choose how to buy it, and ask a question if you need help.

The roasting process

Roasted close to the coffee.

Atlas is roasted on a hand-built Cormorant CR600g, made in Cornwall. Its small-batch capacity suits the way I want to work: hands-on, deliberate, and close enough to each roast to make careful adjustments.

The equipment is not the whole story. It simply gives the process the control and repeatability needed to make better decisions for the cup.

What that means for you

Coffee that is roasted with attention, described honestly, and made to taste good in the cup—not just sound impressive on paper.
