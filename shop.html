Peru Cajamarca

A bright, lifted Peruvian with panela, vanilla, cooked citrus and soft fruit sweetness. Light, lively and gently winey, with hints of cocoa, almond, plum and sweet cherry.

Best for: Filter drinkers and anyone who enjoys bright, fruity coffees.

  • Fruity & floral
  • Light & lively
  • Best for filter
The Cup

Mellow panela, vanilla, cooked citrus, fresh fruit, and a soft winey acidity.

Coffee Details

This coffee comes from Cajamarca in the northeastern Peruvian highlands, a region known for smallholder production and fertile, high-elevation growing conditions. It is washed and made from a blend of Catimor, Caturra, Bourbon, and Typica varieties, grown at 1600–1900 MASL. The lot is part of a Regional Select programme and is sourced through ongoing relationships with Lima Coffees and producers in northern Peru.

Serra Negra

A smooth, full-bodied Brazilian with praline, milk chocolate and toasted nuts. Comforting, sweet and easy to brew every day, with a gentle lemon-like lift.

Best for: Espresso, cafetière and bean-to-cup drinkers who prefer smooth, chocolatey coffee.

  • Chocolatey & nutty
  • Smooth & rich
  • Great for espresso
The Cup

Praline, mellow milk chocolate, mild acidity, and sweetness.

Coffee Details

Serra Negra is a classic Brazilian coffee from Minas Gerais, produced using the natural process, where the coffee cherries are dried before the beans are removed. It comes from common Brazilian varieties grown at 750–1100 MASL and is shaped by the region’s warm climate, fertile land, and long coffee-growing tradition.
