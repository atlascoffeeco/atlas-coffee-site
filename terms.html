Delivery and collection

We offer UK delivery and free local collection in Redditch. Delivery orders can only be sent to a UK (GB) address. We do not currently ship outside the United Kingdom.

UK delivery is £4.50 on orders under £25, unless the basket includes a 500g or 1kg bag. Delivery is free on 500g and 1kg bags, and on any order of £25 or more. There is one UK delivery rate; we do not offer a faster postage option.

We aim to post delivery orders within 2–4 working days, excluding weekends and bank holidays. Dispatch can take longer when a roast is still in progress or when the courier is delayed.

If you choose collection, we email the exact Redditch address after payment and agree a time that works. Collection is from a small roasting space, not a shop with regular opening hours.

More detail is on our Delivery & Returns page.
