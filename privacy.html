Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18th June 2026

Cookies summary

We use cookies and similar technologies to:

  • make the website work properly using strictly necessary cookies;
  • understand how visitors use the site through Google Analytics 4, but only where you have given consent;
  • support secure payment processing through Stripe and site security and performance through Cloudflare.

We do not currently use marketing or advertising cookies. Please read the Cookies section below for more detail on the cookies we use and how you can manage them.

Who we are

Atlas Coffee is a small-batch coffee business based in the United Kingdom. In this privacy policy, “we”, “us” and “our” refer to Atlas Coffee.

Contact email: atlascoffeeroasters@gmail.com

Business status: Sole Trader

The information we collect

We may collect personal information including your name, email address, phone number, billing and delivery details, order information, collection preferences, and any message you send through our contact form or by email.

We may also collect limited technical information when you use the website, such as IP address, browser type, device information, and website usage data, depending on how the site is configured.

How we collect your information

We collect personal information when you place an order, contact us through the website, email us directly, request local collection, or otherwise interact with Atlas Coffee.

Some technical information may be collected automatically through cookies or similar technologies if these are used on the site.

How we use your information

We use your information to:

- process and fulfil orders;

- arrange delivery or local collection;

- respond to enquiries and provide customer support;

- keep business and accounting records;

- improve the website and protect it from misuse, where applicable.

Our lawful bases

We process personal information where necessary to perform a contract with you, such as fulfilling an order or arranging collection.

We may also process information where necessary for our legitimate interests, such as replying to enquiries, keeping records, preventing misuse of the website, and running Atlas Coffee responsibly.

Where consent is required, such as for certain cookies or marketing communications, we will rely on your consent and you can withdraw it at any time.

Payments

Payments are processed securely by Stripe or another payment provider used on our website. We do not store your full payment card details ourselves.

Stripe may process personal information needed to complete and protect payments, including billing and transaction information. Stripe may also use cookies or similar technologies that are strictly necessary for secure payment processing and fraud prevention.

Who we share information with

We may share personal information with trusted service providers only where necessary to run Atlas Coffee, including payment processors, website hosting providers, email providers, and delivery services.

Where relevant, this may include providers such as Stripe for payments, Cloudflare for hosting, performance and security, and Google for analytics where you have consented to analytics cookies.

We do not sell your personal information.

How long we keep your information

We keep personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for customer service, order fulfilment, legal, tax, and accounting purposes, or to deal with any follow-up issues.

Where information is no longer needed, we aim to delete it or securely dispose of it.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law, you may have the right to request access to the personal information we hold about you, ask for inaccurate information to be corrected, ask for information to be deleted, restrict or object to certain processing, and request transfer of your data where applicable.

If we rely on consent, you also have the right to withdraw that consent.

Cookies

We use cookies and similar technologies on our website to make it work properly, support secure checkout, understand how visitors use the site, and improve our services where permitted.

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They help websites remember actions and preferences, maintain sessions, improve security, and measure how the site is used.

Types of cookies we use

We use the following categories of cookies:

- strictly necessary cookies, which are needed for the website, basket, checkout, payment handling, and security features to work properly;

- analytical cookies, which help us understand how visitors use our website and improve its performance, but only where you have given consent;

- we do not currently use marketing or advertising cookies for targeted advertising.

Strictly necessary cookies

These cookies are essential for the operation of the website and do not require consent. They may be used to keep your basket active, maintain session state, support checkout, process payments securely, and protect the website from misuse or malicious traffic.

Analytics cookies

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand how visitors use our website. Google Analytics 4 may use first-party cookies, including cookies such as _ga and _ga_*, to distinguish visitors and maintain session state.

These cookies help us measure things such as page visits, traffic sources, and general usage patterns so we can improve the website. We do not use Google Analytics to directly identify you by name.

Consent for analytics cookies

Where required by law, we will ask for your consent before setting non-essential analytics cookies on your device. If you decline analytics cookies, they should not be set, and the website will continue to function using only strictly necessary cookies.

If you later change your mind, you can update your cookie preferences through the cookie controls made available on the site, if enabled, or by clearing cookies in your browser and revisiting the site.

Google Tag Manager and consent mode

We use Google Tag Manager to manage certain website tags, including analytics-related tags. We may also use Google consent settings or consent mode so that analytics storage is only enabled where the appropriate consent choice has been made.

Google Tag Assistant may be used by us for testing and debugging our tag setup. It is a diagnostic tool and is not itself listed as a separate cookie service in this policy.

Stripe and payment-related technologies

When you make a purchase, Stripe or another payment provider may use cookies or similar technologies that are strictly necessary for payment processing, fraud prevention, and secure checkout functionality.

Cloudflare and security/performance technologies

Our site may also use Cloudflare or similar infrastructure providers to improve security, performance, and reliability. These providers may set cookies or use similar technologies that are strictly necessary to manage traffic, detect malicious activity, and support the secure delivery of the website.

Managing cookies

You can manage or delete cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers let you block cookies, delete cookies already stored on your device, or clear them when you close your browser.

Please note that blocking strictly necessary cookies may affect the functioning of the website, including basket and checkout features.

More information

For more information about how Google uses data and cookies, please visit Google’s cookies policy.

For more information about Stripe’s privacy practices, please visit Stripe’s privacy policy.

For more information about Cloudflare’s privacy practices, please visit Cloudflare’s privacy policy.

Data security

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information and limit access to it to what is necessary for running the business.

How to complain

If you have any questions or concerns about how we use your personal information, please contact us first at atlascoffeeroasters@gmail.com.

You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO) if you believe your data has been handled unlawfully. More information is available at ico.org.uk.

You can also read the ICO’s public guidance on cookies and online tracking at ICO cookies guidance.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the website, the way Atlas Coffee operates, or legal requirements. The latest version will always be published on this page.
