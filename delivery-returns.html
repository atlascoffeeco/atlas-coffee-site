Delivery & Returns

Last updated: 31 August 2026

How orders are fulfilled

Atlas Coffee is a small-batch coffee shop based in Redditch. You can have coffee posted within the United Kingdom, or collect it locally in Redditch. You choose delivery or collection in the basket before checkout.

There are no customer accounts. Payment is taken on Stripe’s checkout pages. Delivery orders can only be sent to a UK (GB) address.

UK delivery

There is one UK delivery charge:

  • £4.50 on orders under £25, unless the basket includes a 500g or 1kg bag;
  • free on 500g and 1kg bags;
  • free on any order of £25 or more.

The basket shows whether delivery applies before you pay. We do not offer a faster or more expensive postage option.

We do not currently ship outside the United Kingdom.

Dispatch

Coffee is roasted and packed in small batches. We aim to post delivery orders within 2–4 working days, excluding weekends and bank holidays.

Dispatch can take longer when a roast is still in progress, or when the courier is delayed. We will email you if we need to confirm anything about your order.

Local collection

Collection is free in Redditch. Choose it in the basket if you are local and able to come by.

After you pay, we email the exact address and agree a time that works. Collection is from a small roasting space, not a high-street shop with regular opening hours.

Your right to cancel

If you buy online as a consumer, you will usually have the right to cancel within 14 days of receiving the coffee, without giving a reason. You then usually have a further 14 days to return it.

If we have not yet posted the order or handed it over for collection, email us and we will usually cancel it and refund you without you needing to send anything back.

Please email atlascoffeeroasters@gmail.com before posting anything to us, and include your name, order details, and a short description of what you would like to do.

Refunds after cancellation

Where a cancellation right applies, we will usually refund you within 14 days of receiving the coffee back, or receiving evidence that it has been sent back.

If you paid the £4.50 UK delivery charge, that amount is refunded as well. Collection orders have no delivery charge to refund.

Exceptions

Some goods may not be eligible for cancellation or return where they are sealed for health protection reasons and have been unsealed, or where they are liable to deteriorate or expire quickly.

That may apply to coffee, depending on the condition of the bag and the circumstances of the return. It does not take away your rights if the coffee is faulty, not as described, or not fit for purpose.

Damaged, faulty or incorrect orders

If your order arrives damaged, faulty, or incorrect, please contact us as soon as possible at atlascoffeeroasters@gmail.com.

Where goods are faulty, not as described, or not fit for purpose, your statutory rights under UK consumer law still apply.
