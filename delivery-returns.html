Delivery & Returns

Last updated: 25 May 2026

Delivery

Atlas Coffee delivers within the United Kingdom.

UK delivery is £4.50 on orders under £25. Delivery is free on 500g and 1kg bags, and on any order of £25 or more. Free local collection is available in Redditch.

Dispatch

Orders are roasted and packed in small batches. We aim to post orders within 2–4 working days, excluding weekends and bank holidays. Dispatch can take longer when a roast is still in progress or when courier services are delayed.

Local collection

Free local collection is available in Redditch.

Full collection details can be confirmed after your order is placed.

Your right to cancel

If you buy online as a consumer, you will usually have the right to cancel your order within 14 days of receiving your goods, without giving a reason.

If you cancel within this period, you then usually have a further 14 days to return the goods to us.

Refunds after cancellation

Where a cancellation right applies, refunds will usually be made within 14 days of us receiving the returned goods back, or receiving evidence that they have been sent back.

If standard delivery was paid for, that cost will also be refunded. If a more expensive delivery option was selected, we only refund the standard delivery cost.

Exceptions

Some goods may not be eligible for cancellation or return where they are sealed for health protection reasons once unsealed, or where they are liable to deteriorate or expire quickly.

This may apply to coffee and other food products depending on the condition of the goods and the circumstances of the return.

Damaged, faulty or incorrect orders

If your order arrives damaged, faulty, or incorrect, please contact us as soon as possible at atlascoffeeroasters@gmail.com.

Where goods are faulty, not as described, or not fit for purpose, your statutory rights under UK consumer law will still apply.

How to contact us about a return

Please email atlascoffeeroasters@gmail.com with your name, order details, and a short description of the issue before sending anything back.

Changes to this page

We may update this page from time to time to reflect changes to fulfilment, delivery methods, or legal requirements. The latest version will always be published here.
