About Atlas Coffee

A small-batch roaster with a focus on single-origin coffee.

Atlas Coffee is built around a simple idea: roast carefully, source coffees with character, and make it easy for people to enjoy fresh coffee at home. The focus is on single-origin coffees that show balance, clarity and a distinct sense of place.

How Atlas Coffee roasts

Each coffee is roasted in small batches with the goal of bringing out sweetness, balance and the best qualities of the origin. The intention is not to hide the coffee behind the roast, but to let the cup stay expressive, clean and approachable.

Single-origin selection

Coffees are chosen for flavour, quality and traceability.

Careful roasting

Small-batch roasting helps keep each release fresh and consistent.

Simple ordering

Customers can order for delivery or local collection from Church Hill North, Redditch.

What the brand stands for

Atlas Coffee aims to feel modern, independent and straightforward. The brand is about good coffee without unnecessary clutter: clear tasting notes, thoughtful roasting, and a buying experience that feels easy from first visit to first brew.
